Mozilla Rhino 1.9.1 Source Availability
========================================

The Scriptella binary and examples distributions include the following
Mozilla Rhino 1.9.1 artifacts under the Mozilla Public License, version 2.0:

* org.mozilla:rhino-engine:1.9.1
* org.mozilla:rhino:1.9.1

The exact corresponding source artifacts are available from Maven Central:

https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/mozilla/rhino-engine/1.9.1/rhino-engine-1.9.1-sources.jar
https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/mozilla/rhino/1.9.1/rhino-1.9.1-sources.jar

The complete MPL 2.0 license text is provided in rhino.license.txt.
